About

Hi, we're William & Summer.

We're both current freshmen at Thomas Jefferson High School for Science and Technology (TJHSST) — we just went through the admissions process ourselves, so we know what it actually takes, not just what it looks like from the outside.

We tutor students together in the Northern Virginia / TJHSST area on weekends, covering everyday math coursework as well as TJ admissions prep for students working toward the same goal.
